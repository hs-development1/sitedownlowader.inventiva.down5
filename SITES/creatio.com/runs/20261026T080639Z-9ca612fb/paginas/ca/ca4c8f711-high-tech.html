Campaign management

Engage your customers by designing, executing, and optimizing omnichannel marketing campaigns.

Event management

Increase brand awareness and tap into high-potential lead pools by executing effective marketing campaigns tailored for industry events and trade shows.

Digital marketing

Efficiently orchestrate diverse digital marketing activities, such as SEO, SMM, online brand promotion, video production, and others.

Account based marketing

Maintain synchronization of all account based marketing activities and data, analyze account engagement history, and manage complex account based campaigns.

Marketing collateral management

Streamline development of marketing materials, set up version control, and organize single source access to marketing collateral.

Sales

Lead generation

Automate lead generation activities via integrations with social media engines and website landing pages, keep lead generation and sales teams' activities and data synchronized.

Opportunity management

Increase win rates by automating workflows for different types of sales opportunities, such as product, service, outsourcing/outstaffing, etc.

Partner relationship management

Drive partner sales through a portal that supports leads and deals collaboration, mutual campaigns, knowledge sharing, and loyalty programs.

Account based selling

Effectively manage multiple contacts within a target account, offer personalized content and value propositions depending on the contact role, implement upselling and cross-selling workflows for high-tech products/solutions.

Sales forecasting

Create and manage forecasts in multiple dimensions by teams, territories, products/services, and other parameters.

Project Management

Delivery management

Keep track of delivery milestones, phases, and activities, and ensure daily completion of tasks by using your favorite SDLC methodology.

SOW & project documentation

Easily maintain project documentation up to date, manage access rights, and share with the stakeholders and the team.

Team management

Manage complex, distributed teams and keep track of their performance, integrate multiple team management methodologies and task management systems as needed.

Customer communications

Manage customer communications within one system, easily share status updates and create meeting notes, and keep the stakeholders in the loop of the essential delivery activities.

Business Analysis

Requirements management

Set up transparent workflows for requirements management and maintain a single source for requirements documentation.

Change management

Increase delivery agility and embrace change by automating change management processes and speeding up customer feedback loops.

Stakeholder management

Keep the stakeholders always informed, easily gather feedback, and store all important communications from multiple channels in one system.

Backlog & solution roadmap

Eliminate project bottlenecks, gold plating, or scope creep by continuously maintaining feature specs, backlog, solution roadmap, etc. up-to-date and approved by the stakeholders and the team.

Product Management

Market & competition analysis

Collect and organize knowledge on market trends, competition, analyst reports, etc. to adjust the product development strategy.

Product backlog management

Set up automated workflows for product backlog development, review, prioritization, and sign-off, and ensure delivery teams always get accurate data on the product strategy.

User & feedback analytics

Collect, analyze, and visualize user behavior data and feedback.

Release management

Implement effective workflows for planning, scheduling, and rolling out of new product releases that incorporate all necessary activities on quality assurance, DevOps, customer acceptance, and technical support.

Knowledge management

Improve knowledge sharing and build up technical expertise faster via across-the-board automated knowledge management workflows.

ROI management

Keep track of financial expenses and ensure effective allocation of resources by implementing ROI management dashboards, KPI analytics, OKR analytics, and other strategic management workflows.
