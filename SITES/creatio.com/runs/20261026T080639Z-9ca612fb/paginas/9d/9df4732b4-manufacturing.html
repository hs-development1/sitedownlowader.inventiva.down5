Lead management

Campaign management

Engage your customers by designing, executing, and optimizing omnichannel marketing campaigns.

Trade promotions

Increase channel participation by providing a seamless experience of managing end-to-end trade promotion cycles.

Channel rebates and MDFs

Sales & Distribution

Opportunity management

Increase win rates by automating workflows for different types of sales opportunities (B2C, B2B, B2B2C).

Partner relationship management

Easily manage your contacts and accounts via a single database and enjoy a 360-degree view into your customers' and partners' profiles, history, and engagement status.

Field visits

Boost efficiency of field sales activities via a mobile app for scheduling, routing, visit execution, order placement, and reporting.

E-commerce orders processing

Grow the number of online orders and customer satisfaction through automation of freight and service order placement, as well as validation.

Sales forecasting

Create and manage forecasts in multiple dimensions by divisions, territories, load types, and other parameters.

Quotes, Orders, and Invoices

Product catalog and configuration

Keep product information aligned using a digital catalog, easily create product bundles that factor in different price lists, margins, and other parameters.

Quote management

Ensure accurate generation of quotes and invoices based on single-source product and pricing data, with support from business rules.

Order processing

Deliver timely order fulfillment by automating diverse order processing workflows.

Billing and invoicing

Generate invoices in seconds, track their status, and get complete visibility into the payment flow.

Production Management

Product lifecycle management

Resource assignment

Work order management

Streamline day-to-day operations by effectively managing work orders, schedules, documents, and maintenance tasks.

Performance monitoring

Gain a comprehensive visibility into your real-time and historical performance data based on a 360-degree view of your production assets and workflows.

Channel rebates and MDFs

Procurement

Request management

Orchestrate purchasing plans, requests, and forecasts via automated workflows that ensure that any needs are properly submitted, tracked, and executed.

Vendor management

Build stronger relationships with partners, suppliers, and contractos by supporting their diverse needs via a digital self-service portal.

Procurement lifecycle management

Integrate your procurement lifecycle into a set of automated and highly transparent workflows while ensuring across-the-board data synchronization.

RFx management & contracting

Supply Chain Management

Supply chain planning & tracking

Ensure that all product input is properly timed and tracked, automate distribution workflows, and minimize production downtime via automated delivery processes.

Shipment lifecycle management

Automate shipment scheduling to avoid delays, track goods and fleet en route, and keep your inventory and delivery data synchronized.

Inventory planning & control

Automate product data sharing and syncronization between multiple divisions, and avoid over-/understocking in warehouses.

Risk & Compliance Management

Customer privacy management

Regulatory compliance control

Legal case management

Streamline management of legal cases, ensure unified format for documents, and optimize legal-related workflows and collaboration process.

Certification development

Track, monitor, and manage certification processes throughout the entire organization to ensure regulatory compliance.

Internal audit

Manage audit workflows across all required financial, physical, and legal assets within your organization.

Corporate Services

Employee lifecycle

Employee request management

Collaboration & knowledge sharing
