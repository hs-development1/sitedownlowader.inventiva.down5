Switching PR Providers: 5 Actionable Tips for Fintechs

  • Evaluate Fit: Prioritize providers with proven fintech expertise. Look for case studies and client testimonials specific to your niche (e.g., DeFi, payments, regtech).
  • Scrutinize Contracts: Pay close attention to termination clauses, service level agreements (SLAs), and data ownership. Ensure clear deliverables and reporting metrics.
  • Measure Mid-Switch: Establish baseline metrics before switching. Track key performance indicators (KPIs) like media mentions, sentiment, and website traffic during the transition.
  • Set Realistic Timelines: Fintech PR often requires navigating complex regulatory landscapes. Expect a ramp-up period of 1-3 months for new providers to gain traction.
  • Avoid Common Pitfalls: Don't switch solely on price. Ensure cultural alignment and clear communication channels. Avoid abrupt changes to messaging or target audiences.

Common Pitfalls When Selecting PR Support

  • Hiring an agency without deep fintech expertise, leading to messaging that misses critical industry nuances.
  • Experiencing a bait-and-switch where senior staff pitch but junior staff execute, diminishing quality.
  • Setting unrealistic expectations for immediate, high-profile media placements without a solid strategy.
  • Failing to establish clear objectives and measurable KPIs, making it impossible to gauge agency performance.
  • Not providing agencies with sufficient business insights, hindering their ability to craft effective narratives.
  • Selecting an agency whose service model doesn't match the company's current stage, from startup to enterprise.
