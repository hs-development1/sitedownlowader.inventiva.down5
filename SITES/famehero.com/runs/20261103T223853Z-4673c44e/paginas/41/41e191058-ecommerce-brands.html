Transitioning from Traditional PR Agencies

  • Evaluate Fit: Prioritize providers with proven ecommerce experience. Look for case studies demonstrating success with brands similar in scale and sector.
  • Contract Scrutiny: Clearly define deliverables, KPIs, and reporting frequency. Watch for auto-renewal clauses and exit strategies.
  • Measure Results: Establish baseline metrics (website traffic, conversion rates, social engagement) before switching. Track these closely post-transition.
  • Timeline Realism: Expect a 1-3 month ramp-up period for new providers to understand your brand and implement strategies effectively.
  • Avoid Pitfalls: Don't switch solely on price. Ensure cultural fit and clear communication channels are established early on.

PR Agencies: Common Pitfalls for Ecommerce Brands in 2026

  • Misaligning PR goals with business objectives: Many ecommerce brands engage PR without a clear understanding of how it will directly impact sales, customer acquisition, or brand loyalty. This leads to wasted resources on activities that don't move the needle.
  • Choosing the wrong agency fit: Selecting an agency that lacks specific ecommerce experience or a deep understanding of your niche can result in generic strategies and missed opportunities. It's crucial to find partners who "get" your market.
  • Underestimating the time and effort required: PR is not a set-it-and-forget-it solution. Brands often fail to allocate sufficient internal resources or provide timely feedback, hindering the agency's ability to execute effectively.
  • Focusing solely on vanity metrics: Chasing press mentions without tracking their impact on website traffic, conversions, or brand sentiment provides a false sense of success. True PR value lies in measurable business outcomes.
  • Failing to integrate PR with other marketing efforts: PR should complement, not operate in a silo. Brands that don't align their PR messaging and campaigns with their social media, content marketing, and advertising efforts miss out on synergistic growth.
  • Expecting immediate, dramatic results: Building brand reputation and securing meaningful media placements takes time. Impatience can lead to premature termination of agency relationships before they have a chance to yield significant returns.
